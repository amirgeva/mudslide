MUDSLIDE {{VERSION}}
Turn-based artillery on slippery ground.

HOW TO PLAY
Run mudslide.exe. Nothing needs to be installed.

Two to four tanks take turns firing at each other across destructible hills.
Set your barrel's angle and your shot's power, read the wind, and fire.
Blasts throw dirt that piles up and slides downhill, and mud balls bury
whatever they land on. The last tank standing wins the round.

In the menu, choose the number of players, whether each one is a human or a
computer opponent (Easy, Normal or Hard), the number of rounds and the wind.
Human players share one keyboard and take turns.

KEYS
  Left / Right, A / D    Aim the barrel
  Up / Down, W / S       Shot power
  Shift                  Fine adjustment while aiming
  Tab, Q / E, 1-4        Choose a weapon
  Space, Enter           Fire
  Esc                    Pause
  F1                     Show all keys
  M                      Sound on / off
  F11, Alt+Enter         Fullscreen

WEAPONS
  1 Shell         Standard round, unlimited
  2 Heavy Shell   Huge blast, 2 per round
  3 Cluster       Bursts into five bomblets at the top of its arc, 2 per round
  4 Mud Ball      Buries whatever it lands on, 3 per round

Your menu choices are saved in %APPDATA%\Mudslide\Mudslide\settings.ini.
