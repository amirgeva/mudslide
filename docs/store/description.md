**Mudslide** is a turn-based tank artillery game where the ground never stays put.

Two to four tanks face off across rolling hills. On your turn, set your barrel's angle and your shot's power, read the wind, and fire. Then watch the landscape react. Every blast tears out a crater and throws the dirt into the air. It rains back down as loose soil that piles up and slides downhill. Undercut a ridge and it collapses, taking any tank perched on top along for the ride.

And then there's the mud. Lob a Mud Ball onto a rival and it lands as a heavy, slumping heap that buries whatever is underneath. A buried tank can still shoot, but its shells have to drill their way out of the muck and lose power as they go.

**Features**
- Local multiplayer for 2–4 players on one keyboard. Play friends, the computer, or any mix of the two.
- Three computer difficulty levels, from forgiving to ruthless
- Destructible, shifting terrain: craters, collapsing hillsides, and landslides of loose soil and mud
- Wind that changes every turn, from dead calm to a howling gale
- Tanks fall when the ground gives way, and a long drop hurts. Destroyed tanks explode, sometimes setting off a chain reaction.
- A fresh map every round across four landscapes: Green Valley, Red Canyon, Frost Ridge and Moon Bog
- Matches of 1, 3, 5 or 10 rounds
- Your last shot is traced on screen while you aim, so you can adjust from it

**Weapons**
- **Shell:** your trusty standard round, unlimited
- **Heavy Shell:** a huge blast that throws dirt everywhere (2 per round)
- **Cluster:** bursts into five bomblets at the top of its arc (2 per round)
- **Mud Ball:** buries whatever it lands on (3 per round)

**Winning**
The last tank standing wins the round. Most round wins takes the match, with kills as the tiebreaker.

**Controls**
- Left / Right or A / D: aim the barrel
- Up / Down or W / S: shot power (hold Shift for fine adjustment)
- Tab, Q / E or 1–4: choose a weapon
- Space or Enter: fire
- Esc: pause
- F1: show all keys
- M: sound on / off
- F11: fullscreen
